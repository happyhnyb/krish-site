Underneath

Five layers that ship with every build.

Most AI projects fail around the model, not in it. These layers are not sold separately; they are part of every build, and they are why the systems above hold up in production.

How a project runs
Orchestration

The control layer that decides what an agent does next. Session state, timeouts, fallbacks and retries are planned paths, so a slow API or a dropped call never becomes a surprise.

LLM orchestration, agent design, RAG, OpenAI, Neo4j

Backend

The APIs and integrations that turn model output into real actions: bookings, replies, CRM records and transactions in the tools you already run.

Python, FastAPI, Node.js, REST and GraphQL, Twilio

Infrastructure

Deployment and operations built for real traffic and concurrency, in our cloud or inside your own environment, with monitoring to show when something needs attention.

Docker, Kubernetes, PostgreSQL, Redis, AWS EC2 and RDS

Data and forecasting

Pipelines that keep the data under an AI system fresh and trustworthy, and forecasting where a decision depends on what happens next, with uncertainty shown rather than hidden.

Python, Node.js, MongoDB, government and market data APIs. Price forecasting case study

Requirements

Before any build, the workflow, stakeholders, constraints and risks are mapped and agreed, so technical risk surfaces while it is still cheap to fix.

IREB-certified requirements engineering, system architecture
