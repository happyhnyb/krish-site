About

Two engineers who scope, architect and build every system.

Avara Labs is an AI systems engineering practice founded by Krish Shah. It builds voice agents, knowledge systems, workflow automation and forecasting tools for teams that need them to work in production. It is senior-led by design: you work directly with the engineers who scope, architect and build each system.

Senior-led

The people who scope a system build it.

  • Nothing lost in hand-offs. There is no sales team passing notes to engineers. The context from your first call goes straight into the architecture.
  • Requirements done properly. IREB-certified requirements engineering means the workflow, stakeholders and constraints are agreed before a line of code is written.
  • Named people accountable. From model choice to deployment, the same two engineers own the decisions and answer for the result, including the parts that did not go to plan.
