Questions

What clients ask before writing in

Who will we actually work with?

Krish Shah and Dhairya Pareek, directly. Krish leads requirements and architecture; Dhairya leads data, forecasting and integrations. The people who scope the system build it.

Do we need to replace the tools we already use?

No. Systems are built around your existing CRM, telephony, inbox and data. Where an off-the-shelf component is the right answer, we use it.

How is our data handled?

Access and compliance rules are enforced inside the system, with audit logging where it matters. Sensitive deployments can run inside your own environment; a previous insurance deployment runs on-premises with encryption at rest.

Who owns what gets built?

You do. The code, configuration and documentation are yours, handed over in a state your own team can run.

What if AI is not the right answer?

Then we will say so. Some problems are better solved with a rule, a form or a process change, and the scoping step is designed to find that out early.

What happens after launch?

Systems are monitored in production, thresholds are tuned against real usage, and ongoing support is agreed as part of the scope.
