How we work

Every project runs in four stages.

01

Clarify the real problem

Identify constraints, stakeholders, risks and hidden assumptions before a line of code is written.

02

Design a lean MVP architecture

Define interfaces, data flows and AI boundaries before building, so every later decision is faster and less risky.

03

Build for scale and observability

Structure systems for modularity, iteration and debugging from day one, so they can be improved in production.

04

Deliver, validate and refine

Validation continues past launch, because the best systems improve after deployment.

How a project runs in detail
Why us

Six working rules teams hire us for.

Most AI projects do not fail on the model. They fail on everything around it: unclear requirements, brittle integrations, and no plan for the cases the model gets wrong. That is the part we are built for.

01

Built for production, not for demos

Our systems run inside real workflows: live phone calls, regulated documents, customer inboxes. Every design decision is tested against how the system behaves in use, not how it looks in a pitch.

02

Architecture before code

We map requirements, interfaces and data flows before building. IREB-certified requirements engineering surfaces technical risk early, while it is still cheap to fix.

03

Control is designed in

Compliance rules are enforced at retrieval, not patched on after generation. Uncertain cases route to a person. Failure paths, timeouts and retries are first-class parts of the system.

04

It fits the stack you already run

Systems are structured around your existing teams, tools and CRM. Where an off-the-shelf component is the right answer, we use it instead of building a platform you do not need.

05

One team from model to deployment

Intelligence layer, APIs, infrastructure and delivery are handled together, so nothing gets lost between a prototype and the thing that actually ships.

06

Straight answers

Each of our case studies states what worked, what the limits were, and what we would approach differently today. You get the same candour during a project.
